Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and a password, which is stored only in hashed form. When you use Spendly, we also store the expense records you enter, including amount, category, date, and an optional description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to create and maintain your account, show your expenses and spending summaries, and keep the service working. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a SQLite database on the server running Spendly. Your expense records are linked to your account and are removed when your account is deleted. Please keep your login credentials confidential.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic request information such as your IP address according to Google's own privacy policy. We do not share your expense data with any other third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team through the channel where you accessed the service.
